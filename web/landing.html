COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my personal data stored?
All your messages, diary notes, calendar events, tasks, and vault documents are stored 100% locally in your browser's persistent storage (IndexedDB/localStorage). No data is sent to external servers unless you perform an explicit Google Drive export or call the Gemini AI API.
How do I configure my social media accounts?
Open Settings (⚙️) -> "Social Accounts" tab or click the "⚙️ Accounts" button inside the Social Studio tab. Enter your API keys and channel tokens for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, X, and LinkedIn. Test connections in real-time with one click.
Can I install My Lyfe as a native phone app?
Yes! My Lyfe is a certified Progressive Web App (PWA). You can tap "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen" on iOS and Android to get full offline support, app drawer launch, and native biometric lock.
How do I delete entries like tasks, events, or diary logs?
Every card features a quick ✕ delete button, or you can click any entry to open its edit dialog and tap the red "🗑️ Delete" button with instant safety confirmation.
