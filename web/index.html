My Lyfe — Privacy Policy

Google Play Store & GDPR/CCPA Compliance Disclosure

1. Data Ownership & Privacy

My Lyfe operates on a strict local-first architecture. Your journal entries, chat threads, documents, tasks, and calendar events are stored directly on your personal device using encrypted local databases and Web Storage. We do not sell, track, or share your data with any third parties.

2. Google Authentication & Drive Permissions

When you connect your Google Account, My Lyfe utilizes Google Identity Services exclusively for verifying your account identity and Google Drive app-data folder scope for storing your encrypted backup file (ledger_backup_vault.json). No personal data is accessed outside of your explicit backups.

3. Biometric Data Protection

Biometric authentication (Touch ID, Face ID, Fingerprint) is executed exclusively through your device's secure enclave / WebAuthn API. My Lyfe never reads, captures, or transmits biometric raw data.

4. Data Deletion & Export Rights

You retain 100% control over your data. You can delete all records, export full JSON archives, or wipe local application cache at any time with zero retention.
